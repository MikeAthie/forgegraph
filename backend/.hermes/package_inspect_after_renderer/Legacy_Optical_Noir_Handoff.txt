Strategic Direction
Legacy Optical Noir should launch as a sharp, premium weekend social package centered on sunglasses as the visual product hero. The tone should feel cinematic, refined, and confident without relying on people, logos, or text embedded in imagery.
The campaign should position the weekend drop as a limited, style-forward visual moment: dark optical surfaces, reflective lenses, polished frames, shadow, contrast, and editorial product staging.

Create a client-ready weekend social launch package that ForgeGraph owns end-to-end, from strategy through asset production, QA, packaging, delivery, and evidence capture.
