Legacy Optical Noir Strategy Brief

Legacy Optical Noir Strategy Brief

Stage: strategy_research Client: Legacy Run: Weekend social launch package Owner: ForgeGraph / Atlas agency pipeline

Strategic Direction

Legacy Optical Noir should launch as a sharp, premium weekend social moment built around contrast, restraint, and product desirability. The campaign should feel editorial and cinematic, with sunglasses presented as the hero object in dark, polished, high-intent environments.

The run must be strategy-led before asset production. Creative, channel, CRM, analytics, QA, delivery, and evidence capture should all trace back to this brief.

Campaign Objective

Create a weekend-ready social launch package that positions Legacy Optical Noir as a confident, style-forward eyewear drop and gives the client a complete delivery bundle suitable for immediate review, approval, and deployment.

Core Message

Legacy Optical Noir is eyewear for a sharper weekend: polished, minimal, cinematic, and built around presence without excess.

Audience

Primary audience: style-conscious adults interested in premium accessories, nightlife, weekend outings, fashion-forward essentials, and elevated everyday presentation.

Secondary audience: gift buyers and brand-aware shoppers looking for a distinctive sunglasses product with a refined visual identity.

Creative Principles

  • Sunglasses must be the clear visual subject.
  • No text inside generated imagery.
  • No logos inside generated imagery.
  • No people inside generated imagery.
  • Visual mood: noir, glossy, editorial, premium, restrained.
  • Avoid clutter, novelty props, fake brand marks, or lifestyle scenes that distract from the product.
  • Assets should work across social posts, story crops, and HTML/PDF presentation packaging.

Department Pipeline

  • Strategy Research
  • Define campaign objective, audience, positioning, visual rules, channel needs, and approval constraints.
  • Brand
  • Translate Optical Noir into a visual system: dark neutrals, high contrast, glass reflections, polished surfaces, minimal copy.
  • Channel
  • Prepare weekend-ready social concepts for feed, story, and lightweight promotional use.
  • CRM
  • Create concise supporting copy for client-owned follow-up, launch reminders, and approval notes.
  • Media Production
  • Use a Codex-backed media worker to generate fresh sunglasses assets only.
  • Required constraints: no text, no logo, no people.
  • Output should be clean PNG-ready visuals suitable for packaging.
  • Analytics
  • Define tracking expectations, content naming, campaign evidence, and post-launch reporting fields.
  • QA
  • Validate asset constraints, package contents, file integrity, client-readiness, and delivery rules.
  • Client Approval
  • Package final materials into the approved client ZIP format.
  • Deliver only through the approved WhatsApp route.

Asset Requirements

Generated media must show premium sunglasses in Optical Noir-inspired settings. Recommended visual directions:

  • Black sunglasses on polished black stone with controlled studio reflections.
  • Sunglasses on smoked glass with soft directional light.
  • Close product detail on dark satin or lacquer surface.
  • Minimal luxury still life with chrome, shadow, and neutral highlights.
  • High-contrast editorial product composition for social feed crop.

Do not include typography, watermarks, logos, faces, hands, models, mannequins, or branded packaging in generated visuals.

Deliverable Package Rules

Client ZIP must contain only:

  • `HTML`
  • `PDF`
  • `PNG`
  • `manifest`

WhatsApp Delivery Rule

Delivery must be sent to Mike only.

WhatsApp message should be brief and concrete:

> Mike, the Legacy Optical Noir weekend social launch package is ready. The ZIP includes HTML, PDF, PNG assets, and manifest only. Please review and confirm approval or edits.

QA Checklist

  • Strategy completed before asset generation.
  • All generated sunglasses assets are fresh.
  • No image contains text.
  • No image contains logos.
  • No image contains people.
  • Client ZIP contains only HTML, PDF, PNG, and manifest files.
  • No Markdown files included in client-facing delivery.
  • WhatsApp delivery addressed to Mike only.
  • Delivery message is brief and concrete.
  • Receipt and evidence are durably captured.
