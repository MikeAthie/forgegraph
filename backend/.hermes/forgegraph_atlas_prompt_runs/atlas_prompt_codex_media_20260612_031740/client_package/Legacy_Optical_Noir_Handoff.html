Legacy Optical Noir Weekend Social Launch Strategy Brief

Run Context

Client: Legacy Campaign: Legacy Optical Noir Weekend Social Launch Stage: `strategy_research` Run Owner: ForgeGraph Delivery Recipient: Mike only, via WhatsApp Client File Rule: Do not send Markdown files to the client.

Strategic Direction

Legacy Optical Noir should launch as a sharp, premium weekend social package centered on sunglasses as the visual product hero. The tone should feel cinematic, refined, and confident without relying on people, logos, or text embedded in imagery.

The campaign should position the weekend drop as a limited, style-forward visual moment: dark optical surfaces, reflective lenses, polished frames, shadow, contrast, and editorial product staging.

Core Objective

Create a client-ready weekend social launch package that ForgeGraph owns end-to-end, from strategy through asset production, QA, packaging, delivery, and evidence capture.

The final client ZIP must include only:

  • HTML
  • PDF
  • PNG
  • Manifest

No Markdown files should be included in client-facing delivery.

Required Department Pipeline

  • Strategy Research
  • Define campaign objective, visual direction, audience assumptions, asset rules, delivery constraints, and QA requirements.
  • Confirm that strategy precedes any asset generation.
  • Brand
  • Translate the Optical Noir concept into a premium visual system.
  • Establish art direction: dark editorial lighting, reflective sunglasses, black/chrome/smoke tones, clean luxury styling.
  • Avoid logos, visible brand marks, text overlays, and people.
  • Channel
  • Prepare weekend-ready social placements.
  • Prioritize assets suitable for Instagram feed, stories, WhatsApp preview, and lightweight client review.
  • Keep social copy concise and concrete.
  • CRM
  • Prepare WhatsApp delivery language for Mike only.
  • Do not route to groups or additional contacts.
  • Keep message brief and specific.
  • Analytics
  • Include tracking-ready manifest metadata: asset names, dimensions, intended channel, format, checksum where available, and generation notes.
  • Preserve evidence for final package contents and delivery.
  • QA
  • Confirm all media follows restrictions:
  • Fresh assets
  • Sunglasses-focused
  • No text in image
  • No logos
  • No people
  • No Markdown in client ZIP
  • Verify HTML/PDF/PNG/manifest only.
  • Confirm ZIP opens cleanly and files match manifest.
  • Client Approval
  • Package approved materials.
  • Deliver ZIP to Mike only through WhatsApp.
  • Capture durable receipt and evidence.

Asset Strategy

Use a Codex-backed media worker to generate fresh product visuals. Assets should be original and created for this run, not recycled from prior campaigns.

Media Rules

Every generated image must follow:

  • Sunglasses as the clear hero subject
  • No people, faces, hands, silhouettes, or body parts
  • No visible logos, trademarks, labels, or brand marks
  • No embedded text, captions, watermarks, or signage
  • No fake Legacy branding inside the image
  • Premium noir styling suitable for a weekend launch

Recommended Visual Concepts

  • Black sunglasses on polished obsidian surface with controlled reflections
  • Smoke-gray lenses under narrow cinematic light
  • Chrome-accent sunglasses on dark velvet or glass
  • High-contrast product close-up with lens reflections
  • Minimal luxury flat lay with case-like shadows, no branded case visible

Client Package Requirements

The final ZIP should be named clearly for client delivery, for example:

`Legacy_Optical_Noir_Weekend_Social_Launch.zip`

Allowed contents only:

  • `index.html`
  • Client-facing PDF presentation or proof
  • Final PNG assets
  • `manifest.json` or `manifest.txt`

Do not include:

  • Markdown files
  • Source prompts
  • Drafts
  • Internal notes
  • Working files
  • Unapproved image generations
