Strategy Narrative

Positioning: Position Legacy Optical Noir as quiet-status eyewear for Mexico City buyers who want a precise, editorial look without loud luxury signaling.

Audience: Style-aware professionals in Roma Norte, Condesa, Juarez, and Polanco who buy fewer but better accessories, Existing optical customers who respond to curated drops, appointment guidance, and WhatsApp concierge support, Gift buyers looking for a premium but practical weekend purchase with fast human assistance

Approach: Use a weekend approval-gated social launch: lead with product photography, send prospects into WhatsApp consultation, and measure saves, replies, profile visits, and appointment intent before scaling media spend.

Requirements shaping the choice: No live publishing claim until channel receipts exist, No text, logos, people, or fake brand marks inside campaign images, Spanish-first copy must stay concrete, premium, and low-hype, Client approval is required before production launch or paid amplification

Key Decisions

1. Make Optical Noir a product-photo-led weekend launch instead of a broad discount campaign.

- Options considered: discount-led urgency, generic lifestyle content, paid-first reach campaign

- Requirements shaping the choice: premium positioning, no unsupported live publishing claim, approval before production

- Agency teams involved: Strategy, Brand Content, QA Compliance, Channel Execution

- Reasoning: Product photography and concierge response preserve premium trust while still creating measurable weekend demand.

- Not recommended: discount-led urgency, unapproved live-publishing claim

2. Use WhatsApp consultation as the conversion path before paid scale.

- Options considered: direct checkout push, influencer-first launch

- Requirements shaping the choice: limited launch proof, need for human fit guidance, approval-gated strategies

- Agency teams involved: CRM, Channel Execution, Analytics

- Reasoning: Human guidance is more credible for premium optical products and gives the team cleaner intent signals.

- Not recommended: checkout-first message without consultation

What Changed

  • The media quality bar moved from placeholder readiness to production-photo readiness. (reasoning: Spec-rendered doodles did not meet the client-ready standard for premium optical retail.; prompted by: visual QA comparison against approved product-photography direction; led by: QA Compliance)
  • The report framing now emphasizes client decisions, launch controls, and next actions. (reasoning: The handoff needs to read like an approval document, not a raw department note.; prompted by: handoff quality review; led by: Client Approval Ops)
  • Client approval refined the work: Held client-ready status until media used real raster generation and the report used a polished client handoff format.

Insights & Learnings

  • Client-ready quality standard: Approved deliverables must separate client-facing handoff from backend provenance and must clearly block placeholder media.
  • Legacy Optical Noir quality learning: Client-ready campaign work needs production-grade media, polished handoff format, provenance in manifests, and explicit approval gates before launch.

Execution Plan

Execution Plan:

Approve the Optical Noir direction and select final hero assets; publish the weekend social set only after approval and channel receipts; route high-intent replies into WhatsApp concierge for availability, fit guidance, and holds; run a 48-hour response window; then use the Monday readout to choose the best-performing angle for retargeting. Handoff assets: strategy report, approval checklist, campaign asset set, WhatsApp response scripts, and measurement plan.

  • Overly polished luxury language could feel generic; keep copy specific to the product and CDMX buying context.
  • Launching without channel receipts would weaken provenance; keep production blocked until approval is recorded.
  • Image quality must remain production-grade; placeholder/spec-rendered assets should trigger a hold rather than client-ready status.
  • Not recommended: discount-led urgency, unapproved live-publishing claim
  • Requirements shaping the choice: premium positioning, no unsupported live publishing claim, approval before production
  • Not recommended: checkout-first message without consultation
  • Requirements shaping the choice: limited launch proof, need for human fit guidance, approval-gated strategies
  • Approval tradeoff: The handoff now distinguishes strategy approval, production launch approval, and channel receipt requirements.

Recommendations
