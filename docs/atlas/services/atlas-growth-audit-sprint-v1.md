# Atlas Growth Audit + 30-Day Action Plan — Service Spec v1

> Client-first product spec for Atlas/ForgeGraph. This document defines the sellable agency offer before implementation details.

## 1. One-line promise

Atlas helps Mexico City small businesses find and convert new customers with a free growth audit, a practical 30-day action plan, and optional done-for-you execution paid primarily through commission on new business.

Spanish client-facing version:

> Atlas ayuda a pequeños negocios en CDMX a conseguir nuevos clientes con una auditoría gratuita, un plan de crecimiento de 30 días y ejecución opcional pagada principalmente por comisión sobre nuevos ingresos.

## 2. Target customer

Initial market:

- Mexico City only.
- Friends/family/warm introductions first.
- Upper-class or upper-middle-class small-business owners.
- 5–10 employee businesses.
- Not currently paying a marketing agency.
- Low or zero upfront marketing budget.
- Owner trusts Mike/Atlas enough to permit experimentation.
- Business has enough margin/capacity that one new client or sale can be meaningfully valuable.

Example verticals:

- Restaurants and bars.
- Cleaning services.
- Local services.
- Specialty retail.
- High-ticket niche products.
- Zombie/underused projects like Legacy glassware.

Important assumption:

The client will be minimally involved after intake. Atlas should plan to produce media, copy, landing pages, ads, tracking, outreach, and follow-up systems ourselves rather than depending on the business to execute.

## 3. Core business model

### Free audit

Atlas offers the initial audit for free to reduce friction and build trust.

The audit should make the owner think:

- “They understood my business.”
- “There are obvious things we are not doing.”
- “If this works, paying commission is easy.”

### Commission on new business

Default commercial model:

- No or low upfront fee.
- Atlas earns a percentage of profit from new business it sources or materially influences.
- Conservative baseline: 20% of profit from incoming new business.
- Strong warm-network deals may support 30–50% of profit, especially when the business has no existing sales motion.

Legacy example:

- Legacy has no meaningful current marketing/sales motion.
- If Atlas produces a new sale, Atlas may capture up to 50% of profit from that sale.
- 20% of profit is conservative for friends/family pilots.

### Paid services upsell

Atlas can also offer fixed-fee implementation services when useful:

- Landing page.
- Brand refresh.
- Product photography / media package.
- Social content pack.
- Paid ads setup.
- WhatsApp sales funnel.
- Google Maps/local SEO setup.
- CRM/follow-up setup.

Positioning:

> The audit is free. We only make money if we create value. If you want us to build assets faster, we can also quote specific paid services.

## 4. Client promise

English internal version:

The first client-facing package should be in Spanish.

### Main deliverables

1. Resumen ejecutivo.
2. Diagnóstico del negocio.
3. Oportunidades de crecimiento.
4. Revisión de presencia digital.
5. Perfil del cliente ideal.
6. Oferta/promoción recomendada.
7. Plan de acción de 30 días.
8. Quick wins de 7 días.
9. Propuesta de comisión / modelo de trabajo.
10. Lista de activos que Atlas puede producir.
11. Plan de medición simple.

### Package formats

- PDF polished report.
- Markdown/source version for internal editing.
- Manifest/provenance package for ForgeGraph.
- Optional future: email handoff artifact.

## 6. Universal audit structure

This service should work across verticals. The questions and recommendations adapt, but the structure stays stable.

### Section 1 — Contexto del negocio

Purpose:

Understand what the business sells, where, to whom, and why it matters.

Questions:

- ¿Qué vende el negocio?
- ¿En qué zona de CDMX opera?
- ¿Quién compra hoy?
- ¿Cuál es el producto/servicio más rentable?
- ¿Cuál es el ticket promedio?
- ¿Cuál es el margen estimado?
- ¿Cuánta capacidad extra tiene?

### Section 2 — Cliente ideal

Purpose:

Define who Atlas should try to acquire.

Questions:

- ¿Qué tipo de cliente deja más utilidad?
- ¿Qué cliente es más fácil de cerrar?
- ¿Qué cliente genera recompra o referidos?
- ¿Qué zonas/colonias son prioritarias?

### Section 3 — Oferta inicial

Purpose:

Create a concrete offer/promo that can be advertised or sold directly.

Examples:

- Restaurant/bar: evento, promo de grupo, menú especial, experiencia premium.
- Cleaning: limpieza inicial con descuento, paquete recurrente, limpieza post-obra, oficina pequeña.
- Legacy/glassware: paquete de vasos para bares/restaurantes, regalo corporativo, pedido personalizado.

Output:

- Oferta recomendada.
- Precio/rango sugerido.
- Razón por la que debería convertir.
- Objeciones esperadas.
- Cómo responderlas.

### Section 4 — Canales de adquisición

Purpose:

Pick channels Atlas can execute without depending heavily on the client.

Channel options:

- WhatsApp outbound/warm outreach.
- Instagram content and DMs.
- Google Maps/local search.
- Landing page + lead form/WhatsApp.
- Paid Meta ads with small test budget.
- Partnerships/referrals.
- Direct sales to nearby businesses.

Principle:

Prefer channels where attribution is possible and Atlas can own execution.

### Section 5 — Activos necesarios

Purpose:

List what Atlas must create to sell without client involvement.

Possible assets:

- Landing page.
- WhatsApp scripts.
- Sales one-pager.
- Offer deck.
- Photo/video plan.
- AI-generated/adapted visuals where acceptable.
- Simple brand refresh.
- Ad creatives.
- Instagram posts/stories.
- Google Maps profile improvements.
- CRM/tracking sheet.

### Section 6 — Plan de acción de 30 días

Purpose:

Turn strategy into execution.

Suggested structure:

- Semana 1: setup, tracking, offer, landing/WhatsApp flow.
- Semana 2: first outbound/content/ad tests.
- Semana 3: optimize based on responses.
- Semana 4: double down, report, decide next commission/paid service phase.

### Section 7 — Medición y comisión

Purpose:

Avoid vague success-based pricing.

Define:

- What counts as a lead.
- What counts as a qualified lead.
- What counts as a new client/sale.
- How profit is calculated.
- How Atlas gets attributed.
- When Atlas gets paid.

Recommended default:

- Audit: free.
- Setup/service assets: optional paid fee.
- Commission: 20% of profit from Atlas-sourced new business.
- Warm/friends special cases: negotiate 20–50% depending on who executes and how dormant the project is.

## 7. Minimum intake

Since clients may not be involved much, intake must be short. The initial version should be answerable in 15–20 minutes.

Required fields:

- Business name.
- City/neighborhood in CDMX.
- Business type.
- Main product/service.
- Average ticket.
- Rough profit margin.
- Current monthly sales or rough activity level.
- Capacity for extra customers/orders.
- Existing links: Instagram, website, Google Maps, WhatsApp, menu/catalog if any.
- Best customer type.
- Biggest problem.
- What would count as a successful new client/sale.
- Whether Atlas can contact prospects directly on behalf of the business.
- Who confirms whether a lead/sale closed.

Optional fields:

- Photos/videos.
- Brand assets.
- Competitors.
- Past campaigns.
- CRM/customer list.
- Customer testimonials.

## 8. Client involvement model

Assume low involvement.

### Required involvement

- Initial 15–20 minute intake.
- Permission to use brand/business information.
- Agreement on commission and attribution.
- Confirmation of closed sales/new clients.

### Atlas-owned execution

Atlas should be able to own:

- Audit.
- Offer design.
- Copywriting.
- Landing page.
- Ads setup if budget exists.
- Outreach scripts.
- Content ideas.
- Basic media direction or generated mockups.
- Tracking sheet.
- Follow-up process.

### Client-dependent exceptions

Some items require client cooperation:

- Access to ad accounts.
- Access to Google Maps profile.
- Real photos/videos if needed.
- Fulfillment of the service/product.
- Honest reporting of closed sales.

If these are unavailable, package should include caveats and alternative execution paths.

## 10. Quality bar

The package is client-ready only if it is:

- In Spanish.
- Specific to the business and CDMX context.
- Practical, not generic marketing theory.
- Clear about what Atlas can execute without the client.
- Clear about where client cooperation is required.
- Honest about assumptions and missing data.
- Built around measurable new business.
- Includes a concrete 30-day action plan.
- Includes a commission/tracking proposal.
- Polished enough to send as a PDF.

## 11. Definition of done for v1

A v1 client-ready Atlas Growth Audit is done when:

- Intake data is captured.
- Audit package is generated in Spanish.
- PDF, markdown, manifest, and zip are produced.
- 30-day plan is included.
- Commission/tracking proposal is included.
- At least one Atlas-owned execution path is proposed.
- Operator can review and approve the package.
- Client can receive the package without needing to understand ForgeGraph.
