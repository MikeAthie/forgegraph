Vision summary:

ForgeGraph is an AI Company Operating System. The product should center company creation, company operations, and company command surfaces before any advanced builder model.

Product rule:

If a surface forces the user to think in raw engine or builder terms before they can understand company state, company work, or company decisions, the surface is misframed.
