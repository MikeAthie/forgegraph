# Navigation And IA

Navigation must follow the product doctrine in [canonical-ontology.md](./canonical-ontology.md), [forgegraph-product-definition.md](./forgegraph-product-definition.md), and [company-workspace-model.md](./company-workspace-model.md).

Primary IA direction for the rebuild:

- Company command center is the default entry point.
- Company builder is the guided creation and setup flow.
- Operations, approvals, deliverables, budget, and departments should feel like one company workspace.
- Builder surfaces are secondary to company operations once a company exists.

IA rules:

- Default route should land the user in company operations, not advanced editing.
- Summaries appear before logs.
- Company status, approvals, deliverables, and controls appear before technical traces.
- Compatibility wrappers may remain, but internal route structure should not drive primary UI language.
