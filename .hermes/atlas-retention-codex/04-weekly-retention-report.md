## Goal
Create the weekly proof artifact that keeps clients: what Atlas did, what happened, where the funnel broke, what changes next, and commission/new-business status.

## Scope
- Funnel bottleneck diagnosis.
- Spanish client-facing weekly report.
- Operator scale/kill/iterate recommendation.

## Out of scope
- Live analytics ingestion.
- UI.
- Provider sends.

## Success criteria
- Reports diagnose no-reply, qualified-no-sale, and win/scale scenarios differently.
- Every report has explicit next actions and commission summary.
