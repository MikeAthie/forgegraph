## Goal
Integrate Atlas audit/campaign/lead/weekly artifacts into a concurrent-friendly operator workflow and eventually the whiteboard.

## Scope
- Define backend-owned workflow states/columns.
- Attach campaign, asset, lead, approval, and report artifacts to operator tasks/cards.
- Keep whiteboard as projection, not source of truth.

## Out of scope for first batch
- Drag/drop mutations.
- Client portal.
- Full CRM.

## Success criteria
- Operator can see what each client needs next: intake, audit, campaign design, asset production, ready to launch, live, follow-up needed, review/report, scale/kill, closed/commission.
